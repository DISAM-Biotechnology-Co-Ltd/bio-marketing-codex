# Cosmetics Domain

Typical evidence use: ingredient function, skin/hair mechanism, safety, cosmetic performance and finished-product testing when available.
Typical claim risks: disease treatment, wound-healing/medical claims without authorization, converting cell data into human cosmetic efficacy.
Preferred official regulators: market-specific cosmetic authorities and official guidance.
Default audiences: cosmetic brands, formulators, ODM/OEM partners, ingredient buyers.
Common category error: automatically applying supplement claim rules or treating cosmetic positioning as medical-device efficacy.
