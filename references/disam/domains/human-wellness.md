# Human Wellness Domain

Typical evidence use: human trials, ingredient evidence, safety, nutrition/structure-function rationale.
Typical claim risks: disease treatment/prevention, guaranteed efficacy, ingredient-to-finished-product overreach.
Preferred official regulators: market-specific food/supplement authorities (for example TFDA/MOHW, FDA/FTC, EFSA/EC, SAMR, CAA Japan).
Default audiences: brand owners, formulators, distributors, health-product teams.
Common category error: treating preclinical or single-ingredient evidence as finished-product clinical proof.
