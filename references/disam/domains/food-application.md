# Food Application Domain

Typical evidence use: ingredient function, sensory performance, processing stability, food safety and permitted food-use context.
Typical claim risks: converting food-function evidence into therapeutic health claims or ignoring category/novel-food restrictions.
Preferred official regulators: target-market food authorities.
Default audiences: food manufacturers, R&D teams, flavor/formulation partners and ingredient buyers.
Common category error: treating a food-application ingredient as a supplement or medicine by default.
