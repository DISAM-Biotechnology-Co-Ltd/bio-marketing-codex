# Medical Device Domain

Typical evidence use: intended use, device performance, safety/biocompatibility, clinical/performance evidence applicable to device classification.
Typical claim risks: reusing cosmetic, food or supplement claims without device-specific review; extending authorization beyond intended use.
Preferred official regulators: target-market medical-device authorities such as TFDA, FDA, EU MDR competent sources or NMPA as applicable.
Default audiences: device brands, regulatory teams, OEM/ODM partners and clinical-channel stakeholders.
Common category error: assuming ingredient evidence alone establishes finished-device performance.
