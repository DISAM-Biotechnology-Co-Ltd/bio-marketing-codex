# Agriculture Domain

Typical evidence use: crop trials, agronomic endpoints, soil/plant mechanisms, application rate, safety and regulatory category.
Typical claim risks: unsupported pesticide/disease-control claims, extrapolation across crops or application conditions.
Preferred official regulators: target-market agricultural input and plant-protection authorities.
Default audiences: growers, distributors, agronomists and agricultural enterprises.
Common category error: applying human supplement efficacy language to plant outcomes.
