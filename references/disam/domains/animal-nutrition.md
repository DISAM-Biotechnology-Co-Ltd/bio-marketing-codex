# Animal Nutrition Domain

Typical evidence use: species-specific nutrition, feed/additive evidence, safety, dose and production/health-support endpoints.
Typical claim risks: veterinary treatment claims, cross-species extrapolation and unsupported performance guarantees.
Preferred official regulators: target-market feed, animal nutrition and veterinary authorities.
Default audiences: feed companies, integrators, veterinarians and distributors.
Common category error: copying human-health claims or doses into animal applications.
